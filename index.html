Who We Are

We are a community focused nonprofit initiative built around education, compassion, and support.

The Cognova Initiative is dedicated to helping families understand cognitive conditions and feel less alone during difficult moments. Our goal is to make information easier to access and support easier to find.

Through awareness campaigns, support groups, educational videos, and community partnerships, we work to create a stronger support system for patients, caregivers, and families.
